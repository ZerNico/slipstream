How I approached it

Round one failed because the marks were pictograms of a feature. This round I started from the one verb in the name — slip — and from the product's own 12° slant, and asked which simple, solid forms can carry a displacement in time: a ring whose halves slide past each other, a body that tapers into its own wake, a tile whose notches leave an S. Every mark is built from one or two geometric moves with a single stroke weight, drawn to survive at 24 px, and none uses a chevron, an arrow-head, a lone slanted amber bar or a play triangle. Motorsport stays in the metaphor (drafting, tandem, wake), never in the picture, and live red is left out entirely so it keeps its meaning in the UI.
