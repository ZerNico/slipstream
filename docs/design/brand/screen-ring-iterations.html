A screen,
cut and slipped

Screen Ring was picked. Its weakness was named on the last sheet: straight edges make the slip look like a glitch, and a rounded rectangle is nobody's property. So every card here tries one way of making the move look decided — by proportion, by how far and where it slips, by what the two halves mean — while staying "a TV screen, cut on the 12° slant and slipped". #0 is the version you picked, untouched. Unit u = one stroke width.
