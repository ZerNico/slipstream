Slipstream · brand sheet · v1

Screen Ring

A television screen, cut on the product's 12° slant, its halves slipped past each other by exactly one stroke width. The light half is the broadcast, which has moved on; the amber half is you, one beat behind — live TV that waits for you.

01 · CONSTRUCTION

One screen, one cut, one slip

Everything is derived from the stroke width u = 13.5 on a 100-unit grid. The mark is point-symmetric about the centre of the grid, so it is optically centred without correction.

ScreenOuter 69.5 × 57.5 with radius 23.75; aperture 42.5 × 30.5 with radius 10.25. Between them the picture reads as a soft 4:3 — a television, not a monitor.
CutExactly 12° from vertical, through the centre. Both terminals of each half are parallel to the cut.
SlipEach half moves ½u along the cut in opposite directions: 1u in total, so the top edge of one bar lands on the bottom edge of its neighbour.
Gapu/3 = 4.5, measured square to the cut. It is 1.1 px at 24 px, which is the smallest size of the master.
ColourUpper-left half light, lower-right half amber: the broadcast leads, you follow — and amber ends up beside the wordmark, where the eye lands.
MarginThe mark occupies 66.7 × 70.7 of the 100 grid; the rest of the viewBox is its built-in clear space (≥ 1u).

03 · SIZES

Down to sixteen pixels

The master holds from 24 px upward. Below that, switch to the small variant: stroke 16 instead of 13.5, gap 7 instead of 4.5, and the built-in margin removed so the mark fills its box.

05 · LOCKUPS

Mark and wordmark

SLIPSTREAM is set in Big Shoulders Display 800 and converted to outlines (GPOS kerning applied, tracking +45, seven pairs adjusted by hand). In the horizontal lockup the cap height equals the gap between the mark's outer bars, so the letters sit exactly between them.

Clear space

Keep 1u (one stroke width) free on every side of the mark's footprint and of both lockups. Mark to wordmark is 1.5u horizontally, 1.25u stacked. All SVG files ship with this margin built into the viewBox.

Minimum sizes · shown at actual size

Print: mark 8 mm, horizontal lockup 30 mm wide, stacked lockup 18 mm wide. Nothing below 16 px on screen; below 24 px use the small variant and never a lockup.

06 · MOTION

The slip is the animation

The halves only ever travel along the 12° cut. With prefers-reduced-motion both demos show their end state and do not move.

Intro · 600 ms · cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)

Starts as a whole screen with a hairline cut, then the halves slip one stroke apart and settle. Use on splash, first run and the about screen.

Reaching live · player

When playback catches up with the broadcast, the two halves slide shut into one unbroken screen and hold for a beat — you are live — then the player's own red live badge takes over; the mark itself never turns red.
