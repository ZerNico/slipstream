# Web v2: owner notes for the implementation

Binding input for rebuilding `apps/web` from `final/` and `final/SPEC.md`. These came up after the
synthesis brief was written (2026-11-22) or are about the real build rather than the prototype.

## Mobile must feel native

Seen in the prototype on a 375 × 667 phone (programme sheet "Live F1: Rennen - GP Katar"):

- **Close button collides with the artwork.** On phones, sheets close by dragging down or tapping
  the backdrop; if a close button stays, it sits in the sheet's own header row, never on top of
  artwork.
- **Odd padding above the sheet.** A bottom sheet starts flush with its artwork or header, with a
  visible grab handle and no dead band. Respect `env(safe-area-inset-*)` on every edge.
- **Drag to dismiss did not work.** Bottom sheets need real gesture handling: drag the handle or
  the header (and the content when scrolled to the top) down to dismiss, with velocity-based
  snapping, rubber-banding, and snap points (half / full) where content is long. Use a proven
  primitive (e.g. Vaul-style drawer behaviour on Base UI's Dialog) rather than hand-rolled CSS.
- Test on real phones (iOS Safari and Android Chrome) and in the installed PWA: tap targets
  ≥ 44 px, no hover-only affordances, no accidental horizontal scroll, momentum scrolling inside
  sheets, the keyboard not covering inputs, back gesture / back button closing sheets and dialogs
  before leaving the page.

## Open design issues carried into the implementation (round 4)

- Rule editor › Extra time (#/rules/new, 1440 and phone, admin and non-admin) contradicts itself on owner point 11. The hint reads 'From your default (2 min early, 10 after)', but the steppers show 5 / 60 and the summary at the top says '5 min early and 60 min after'. The rule is on Sky Sport F1 HD, so the Sky Sport F1 HD channel override (5/60) applies. Cause: the hint code (index.html around line 2706) looks only at r.kind for a category override, ignores channel overrides, and a new rule has no kind. Fix: work out the matching default as §7.7 says (channel beats category), the same way for the hint and the stepper values, and name it: 'From your Sky Sport F1 HD default (5 min early, 60 after)'. The programme sheet has the same naming problem: for Qualifying on Sky Sport F1 it says 'From your Sport default' when the channel override should win. The values match there, but the name is wrong. Add an e2e test that the hint's numbers equal the stepper values.
- Recordings › Scheduled on the phone (390x844, sched-390-b.png): the time-column day sub-label 'Mon 23 Nov' under '20:00' now runs under the artwork thumbnail ('Mon 23 Nov' ends behind the tagesschau thumbnail), where in round 3 it wrapped. It also repeats the 'Mon 23 Nov' day header right above it. Decision r3 #11 says the pill notation ('Mon 23') is used everywhere, yet Scheduled and the sheets still use 'Mon 23 Nov'. Fix: drop the day sub-label inside a day group (the header already names the day), or use 'Mon 23' with white-space:nowrap and a column wide enough for it. Use one day notation in the sheets' header and facts too ('Das Erste · Mon 23 Nov 20:00').
- Choice dialog timeline (owner point 5, the main 'join late' flow), at 390x844 and at 844x390. The amber resume knob at 18:11 still sits right on the '18:00' start label, so the label reads '18:0̶0'. See ov-390x844-__choice_r-q.png and ov-844x390-__choice_r-q.png. Also, the dialog's window is 18:00–20:30 while the hero and the player draw the same recording as 17:55–20:30, and 'Watch from start' says 'From 18:00'. Fix: use the recording window (17:55) the same way everywhere. Hide a bottom label when the knob or the live dot is within about 28 px of it, the same way the top labels already hide under 14 %.
- Home on phone and tablet portrait (390, 360, 768x1024, Jonas): 'Needs attention' shows 4 full-width issue cards stacked, about 700 px on a phone and about 1000 px at 768. Continue watching, On now and Scheduled are pushed two or more screens below the hero. For a product whose promise is 'Live TV that waits for you', the first scroll is a wall of problems (crops/390x844-dark-_.png-a.png, hero-768x1024-dark-several.png). At 1440 the same section is a tidy 4-up row. Fix: under 1100 px, show at most one card (the most severe) plus a compact '3 more problems ›' row. Or make the section the same horizontal MediaRow pattern (card width about 300) that every other Home row uses. That also covers owner point 8, consistent rows on Home. Write the rule into SPEC §8.1.
- Recording detail of a running recording breaks owner point 5 and §7.6. On #/recordings/r/r-q (Jonas has a resume position at 18:11) and #/recordings/r/r-borowski, the primary button reads 'Play' and goes straight to ?at=start, and the artwork's play overlay opens #/watch/rec/<id> directly. Neither opens the Resume / From start / Live dialog. Jonas's resume point is ignored even though the hero right above offers 'Resume at 18:11'. The page also shows no Recording state, and its facts claim a final 'Length 1 h 30 min' and 'Exported as /media/tv/…mkv' while capture is still running (detail-r-q.png, rec-detail-nocoll-crop.png). Fix in V.recording: when st==='rec', show a REC badge and make the primary 'Resume at 18:11' or 'Watch' with data-act=choice. Route the art overlay through the choice dialog too. Show Length as 'so far 42 min of 1 h 30 min' and Exported as 'When the recording ends, as /media/tv/…'. Write this case into §8.4 and add it to the choice e2e test.
- Inline 'New collection…' step (programme sheet and rule editor, 1440): the name is prefilled ('Der Pate', 'Live F1'), but focus puts the caret at position 0 with nothing selected (measured selectionStart=selectionEnd=0). Typing a new name prepends to the old one: typing 'Films' gives 'FilmsDer Pate', and Enter then created a collection named 'Der PateEin starkes Team' (inline-after-enter.png). This is the collections-everywhere flow (owner point 12), and it produces garbage names on the first try. Fix: call input.select() after focus so typing replaces the prefill. State this in §8.3 ('name prefilled and selected') and assert it in the inline-step e2e test.
- Phone landscape below 768 wide (667x375) Guide is still open from round 3. - NEW. Home hero in phone landscape below 768 px wide (667x375 iPhone SE, 740x360 and 599x360 Android, 568x320): the tab bar covers all of the hero's actions in every state. Measured: actions at y 315–363 and tab bar top at 311 (667x375). elementFromPoint on 'Resume at 18:11', 'Recording options' and 'Create a rule' returns the Home tab. Fix: in short landscape at any width, either use the compact rail instead of the tab bar (recommended, see the next item), or add `var(--tab-h)` to the hero's bottom padding while the tab bar shows. Add an e2e check at 667x375 and 740x360 that the hero's primary button is the hit target at its own centre.

Show the compact rail (already styled for short landscape) and hide the tab bar at any width in short landscape, so 667x375 behaves like 844x390, which works well. Write this in §2.1/§2.4 and add 667x375 and 740x360 to the e2e viewports for hero, guide and editor.
